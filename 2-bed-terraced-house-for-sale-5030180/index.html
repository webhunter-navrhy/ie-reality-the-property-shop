About the property

Description property

The property Shop are delighted to bring 45 Harold Road to the Stoneybatter market. This Artisan home was well maintained but is in need of some refurbishment. The ground floor of this property consists of a small hallway leading into a large spacious living room, a kitchen with dining area, fully tiled shower room & a sunny yard completes the accommodation on the ground floor, Upstairs there are two spacious bedrooms. Located in the very popular village of Stoneybatter minutes away from an array of independent shops, cafe bars and local restaurants, a real food lover destination. Home of the weekend market at Penders Yard, the Stoneybatter Festival and a community with a great can-do attitude. Also within an easy walk is Smithfield Village, the City Centre, the museum at Collins Barrack, the Phoenix Park and TUD Grangegorman. Great transport links via Dublin Bus, the Luas and Dublin Bikes all add to the appeal of this home. This property offers an ideal opportunity for anyone seeking a city home with the benefit of living in an urban village. Viewing is highly recommended and by appointment only through our Manor Street office.
